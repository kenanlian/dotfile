# YouMind 2026 Case Study

This is a dated example of the general workflow, not reusable product truth. Revalidate every fact before citing it.

## Why the case was difficult

YouMind changed rapidly between 2025 and mid-2026:

- Early materials described `Materials`, `Craft`, and `Chat`.
- A June 2026 structural update changed `Chat` to `Task` and merged `Materials + Craft` into `Files`.
- The 1.0 release expanded the product from research/writing into images, slides, audio/video, webpages, long-term memory, Skills, and external-agent access.
- Founder interviews documented a target shift from professional creators toward broader aspiring/growing creators and a strategic shift from time-saving efficiency to output quality plus a Skill marketplace.

The correct report therefore led with the current 1.0 system and used 2025 sources only for product evolution.

## High-value source order

1. Current homepage and 1.0 release post
2. Updates index and latest structural update
3. Current download/platform page
4. Current privacy policy
5. Current API/agent skill document
6. Recent founder interview for customer and strategy shifts
7. App Store / extension listings and disclosed sponsored demos for workflow confirmation
8. Official incumbent pages for category comparisons

## Dynamic pricing lesson

The visible pricing matrix was client-rendered and the text extractor returned only page chrome. The reliable fallback was to inspect official page source for JSON-LD and embedded application strings, then cross-check official App Store in-app purchases. Use “starts at” for plans with multiple credit bands, and distinguish web from App Store pricing.

## Product-system model used

- **Board:** project context
- **Task:** goal-driven agent execution
- **Files:** source and output artifacts together
- **Sprite:** long-term companion/memory
- **Skills:** reusable and marketable workflows
- **Flow:** capture/research → understand/structure → editable multimodal output → publish/share

This model supported a paradigm comparison against note products (storage/retrieval/knowledge organization) and writing products (drafting/revision/formatting/delivery) without reducing the analysis to checkbox features.

## Evidence cautions

- Treat “with taste,” product superiority, Skill counts, and creator earnings as attributed company claims unless independently corroborated.
- Sponsored YouTube walkthroughs can verify visible workflow details but not comparative superiority.
- Board sharing is not evidence of mature real-time collaboration or enterprise governance.
- Cloud privacy policies and third-party model processing matter when comparing with local-first tools.
- Do not infer an Android or Windows app from “mobile/desktop” marketing; check the current download page.

## Citation concurrency lesson

In the original parallel run, children used the default citation ledger and reset it independently, causing the parent ledger to be repopulated with child sources and ids. The final report remained verifiable only because the parent re-registered and rendered sources afterward. The durable fix is parent-owned ledger assignment: children return raw URLs and evidence, never final citation numbers.
